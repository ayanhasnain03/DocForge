## Principles

1. **Release what you measure** — every OWM card cites the harness version.
2. **Prefer calibration over leaderboard chasing** — RiskCalib sits beside MMLU.
3. **Share negatives** — ablations and failed prompts ship in appendices when possible.
4. **No silent data leakage** — eval items used in training are marked or removed.
